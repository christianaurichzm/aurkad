`arrows.png` and `attackeffect.png`, are drawn by the engine's own
`templates/new-game/build`, under the engine's licence (Apache-2.0).

The sound is CC0 1.0 too, trimmed and levelled for this game. The engine plays
`attack.mp3` by name, and `arrow.mp3` and `magiceffect.mp3` once you add them
for the `archery` and `magic` modules. A map names its own track in `music`.
